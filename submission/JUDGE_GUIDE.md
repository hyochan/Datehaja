# Datehaja — verify the loop

An Agent dates, comes home, and remembers what its owner corrects. Contact
opens only after two independent human yeses.

## Three stops, no account required

1. **[Watch a finished date](https://merry-bass-190.convex.site/watch).** Step
   through all 16 saved lines of Juno and Sol's fictional bookshop encounter.
   Check the journal's source-line chips and both independent Agent letters.
2. **[Compare the same Agent before and after feedback](https://merry-bass-190.convex.site/preview/agent-coaching?lang=en#comparison).**
   Rio keeps polite Korean in later encounters with different partners. The
   English translation sits beside the unchanged original. Open
   [the correction, saved reply and memory](https://merry-bass-190.convex.site/preview/agent-coaching?lang=en#coaching),
   then inspect any of the four dates: 56 lines and three corrections.
   Average reply length did **not** decrease; this is retained guidance, not
   proof of perfect learning.
3. **Check delivery and the runtime.** The
   [delivered email fixture](fixtures/introduction-letter.html) is an actual
   AgentMail message from an earlier fictional development rehearsal, shown
   in the [film](https://youtu.be/-KZKKBA9m-I) at 1:49. It is separate from the
   bookshop record; that public pair cannot receive email.
   In [convex/agentDates.ts](../convex/agentDates.ts), inspect
   `storeTurnAndSchedule` (durable turns and scheduled continuation),
   `deliverDebriefs` (separate owner letters), `get` (private projections),
   and `consent` (transactional two-sided consent). The
   [AgentMail integration](../convex/integrations/agentmail.ts) performs delivery.

Convex also supplies auth, reactive queries and static hosting. OpenAI produces
dialogue, coaching and checked reflections. Firecrawl provides the linked
cultural source; the date can continue if that search returns nothing.

## What the evidence supports

These are saved fictional rehearsals, with playback retimed in the video.
They show an implemented feedback loop, inspectable records and delivered test
email. **Verified independent study participants: 0.** No satisfaction,
retention, demand or real-world chemistry results are claimed.
